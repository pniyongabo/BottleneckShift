\section{Threats to validity}
\label{sec:validity}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{One setup.} All results come from one 0.5B model on one GPU type with
    one vLLM version. A generation step at C1 took about 3.9\ms{} against a 2.2\ms{}
    floor for reading the weights, so host-side and launch work is a large share of
    each step. Larger models, other GPUs, and other serving systems could change the
    results qualitatively as well as in scale.
  \item \emph{Workload.} Closed-loop load bounds queues. Runs of 100 requests start
    with a burst and end with a drain, which inflates mean TTFT at C32
    (Section~\ref{sec:load}). Requests have identical lengths and start together, which
    may favor packing. Prompts are random tokens, and repetitions replay the same
    seed.
  \item \emph{Few repetitions.} Results are medians of three repetitions, which agreed
    within about 1\% for ITL and throughput in most cells; one C8 run had a p99 of
    86\,ms against 120\,ms for the others. Bootstrap intervals over requests are
    narrower than these ranges and understate run-to-run spread. Pooled step
    statistics treat serially correlated steps as independent.
  \item \emph{Timing and clocks.} Step timings are taken at vLLM's scheduler and
    include host-side work; we did not check them against GPU-side timing. GPU clocks
    were not locked: under load the GPU ran near its 140\,W limit, at 1440 to
    1860\,MHz depending on condition and repetition.
  \item \emph{Several machines.} Anchor series reproduced earlier results within about
    1\% on different machines, so machine-to-machine differences were small where we
    could check them.
\end{itemize}
