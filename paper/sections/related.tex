\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

Orca introduced iteration-level scheduling \citep{yu2022orca}, and vLLM added paged
KV-cache management \citep{kwon2023vllm}. Sarathi and Sarathi-Serve introduced chunked
prefill and the per-step token budget, showed that long prefills stall co-scheduled
decodes, and noted that each chunk must read the KV cache of the chunks before it
\citep{agrawal2023sarathi,agrawal2024sarathiserve}; we measure the budget's trade and
the per-chunk cost of context directly in vLLM. Vidur predicts iteration time from
profiled operator costs, modeling prefill attention by prompt length and decode
attention by KV-cache reads \citep{agrawal2024vidur}; our per-chunk measurements are
the kind of data such a model needs for chunked prefill. DistServe and Splitwise run
prefill and decode on separate GPUs to avoid this interference
\citep{zhong2024distserve,patel2024splitwise}; we study it within one engine. Etalon
argues that averaged metrics such as time per output token hide jitter
\citep{agrawal2024etalon}, and our median-versus-tail results are an instance. We
follow Hoefler and Belli's rules for performance measurement
\citep{hoefler2015scientific} in repeating runs, alternating run orders where the
design allowed, and reporting every result, but report no confidence intervals.
Schroeder et al.\ show how closed-loop load generators hide queueing
\citep{schroeder2006open}, which matches our contention and saturation results.
Interference from co-located GPU work is well documented \citep{zhao2025tally}, as is
the weakness of \texttt{nvidia-smi} utilization as a measure of GPU load
\citep{nvidiasmi,baviskar2025gpuutil,frye2025gpuutil}.
