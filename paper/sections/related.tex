\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

Orca introduced iteration-level scheduling \citep{yu2022orca}, and vLLM added paged
KV-cache management \citep{kwon2023vllm}. Sarathi and Sarathi-Serve introduced chunked
prefill and the per-step token budget, showed that long prefills stall co-scheduled
decodes, and noted that each chunk must read the KV cache of the chunks before it
\citep{agrawal2023sarathi,agrawal2024sarathiserve}. Our Phase D and Milestone 5
results measure the budget's trade and the per-chunk cost of context directly in
vLLM. Vidur predicts iteration time from profiled operator costs, modeling prefill
attention by prompt length and decode attention by KV-cache reads
\citep{agrawal2024vidur}; our per-chunk measurements are the kind of data such a model
needs for chunked prefill. DistServe and Splitwise run prefill and decode on separate
GPUs to avoid this interference \citep{zhong2024distserve,patel2024splitwise}; we
study it within one engine. Etalon argues that averaged metrics such as time per
output token hide jitter \citep{agrawal2024etalon}. Our median-versus-tail results are
an instance, and the tail at scale motivates reporting p99 at all
\citep{dean2013tail}. Hoefler and Belli set out rules for interpretable performance
measurement \citep{hoefler2015scientific}. We follow them in repeating runs,
alternating run orders where the design allowed, and reporting every result, but we
report no confidence intervals, which three repetitions cannot support. Schroeder et
al.\ show how closed-loop load generators hide queueing \citep{schroeder2006open},
which matches our contention and saturation results. Interference from co-located GPU
work is well documented \citep{zhao2025tally}, as is the weakness of
\texttt{nvidia-smi} utilization as a measure of GPU load
\citep{nvidiasmi,baviskar2025gpuutil,frye2025gpuutil}; our contention results are one
more instance.
